## What's New in 2.1.4

- Improved PlayStation 1 disc detection for valid fan-made and patched MODE1/MODE2 images whose license signature differs from retail discs, including compatible `.cue/.bin` releases.
- Added Show Information… to the game context menu, displaying ROM metadata and the preferred and available cores for the detected system.

## Fixes

- PlayStation 1 games with a valid ISO9660 filesystem and `SYSTEM.CNF` are now recognized even when the optional retail license text is missing or modified.
